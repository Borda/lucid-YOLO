## WP-033 — toy convergence golden

<a id="wp-033"></a>

MuSGD reaches the 0.1 MSE threshold in **47 steps against momentum-SGD's 90** on a fully seeded micro-CNN regression task.

The tolerance is the interesting part: step counts are pinned at **±15**, final losses at 0.005. Cross-platform torch float drift shifts trajectories slightly ([Float reproducibility](../engineering/cross-cutting.md#cross-float)), so a tight pin would be a golden about the machine. The 43-step separation is what keeps the *directional* claim decisive inside a tolerance that loose — the golden asserts the ordering it cares about rather than the number it happened to get.
