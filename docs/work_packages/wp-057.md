## WP-057 — tiling is what creates difficult instances

<a id="wp-057"></a>

DOTA ships a per-object `difficult` flag and [WP-056](../work_packages/wp-056.md#wp-056) declined to decide its fate at load, deferring to A39. Tiling is where that deferral turns load-bearing: R18's partial-object rule flags an instance difficult when its clipped area falls below 0.7 of its original, so **the tiler manufactures difficult instances the source annotations never carried**. A39 therefore governs a population that DOTA's own flag column does not describe, and the number the decision was made from has to survive the crop — which is why `U` is carried out as `visible_fraction` rather than consumed and discarded.
